Ikon Gardens is a thoughtfully designed celebration space in Nellore, created for moments that deserve to be remembered. From intimate gatherings to grand wedding celebrations, our venue brings together beautiful surroundings, comfortable spaces and a welcoming atmosphere.

Open lawns, soft evening lights and flowering greenery set the scene for weddings, receptions and family celebrations, with room for every guest to feel at home.
