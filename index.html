WELCOME TO IKON GARDENS

A Space for Every Story

Ikon Gardens is a versatile event destination in Nellore, bringing together an elegant banquet hall and spacious open lawns for celebrations of every kind. From weddings, receptions, haldi, mehendi and sangeet ceremonies to birthdays, anniversaries, kitty parties and family get-togethers, we provide a welcoming setting for moments that matter.

Our venue also accommodates corporate gatherings, award ceremonies, team dinners, school events, children's carnivals, food festivals, fashion shows and media events.

Beyond providing the venue, we assist with event planning and coordination, floral and lighting decorations, LED display panels, catering arrangements, additional security, valet parking, and professional photography and videography, including drone coverage. These services can be arranged according to your event requirements, helping you bring your vision to life with greater ease.
